Fire Fusion v6 AUTO · STARTING

Automatic candidate grouping starts when the dashboard opens and re-runs after each successful NASA refresh. Fusion uses reported NASA pixel dimensions and observation times, with a capped group diameter and safeguards against chaining separate hotspots. Ambiguous observations remain separate. Meteosat fire pixels are used to merge groups that sit on the same Meteosat pixel (where covered). Sentinel-2 is review-only and does not merge anything. Event counts are not confirmed fire counts.

Independent imagery review

Meteosat merges only groups sharing one fire pixel, in its coverage area. Sentinel-2 catalogue records cannot prove duplicates.
